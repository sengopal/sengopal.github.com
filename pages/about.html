I am a deep learning researcher and HPC systems engineer, and I enjoy building both the solutions and the teams that solve complex problems. I have led teams of applied scientists and engineers at NVIDIA, Amazon and eBay, and had the good fortune of working on everything from highly available web products and identity platforms to ML models and accelerator runtimes. At AWS I built three teams of more than 20 people for LLM inference on Trainium. We worked on speculative decoding, weight and cache quantization, continuous batching, flash attention, disaggregated inference and hardware-software co-design across compilers, runtimes and collectives, and delivered state-of-the-art latency for open source and Nova LLMs on Amazon Bedrock. At eBay I led applied researchers and engineers on search, visual shopping and embedding models that we scaled to serve a billion queries. I look for research that advances the field and also shows up in production, and I am most interested in how AI systems will scale and perform over the next decade.

I currently lead the NVIDIA Dynamo engineering team focused on performance, along with a team of experts in High-Performance Computing (HPC) who specialize in communication runtimes, parallel programming models, network design, high-speed interconnects and scale-out GPU systems. Dynamo is NVIDIA’s open-source, datacenter-scale inference stack. It runs above SGLang, TensorRT-LLM and vLLM and turns them into one coordinated multi-node serving system. My focus is on communication between GPUs, with high bandwidth, low latency and minimal overhead, so that LLM inference runs at the best possible performance on NVIDIA platforms. I led the development of Dynamo’s Rust runtime services for the 1.0 release at GTC 2026, including request migration and cancellation, graceful shutdown, router metrics and the configuration system shared by every backend. My team also leads Dynamo performance optimizations, fault tolerance for production deployments, and support for new hardware platforms.

Before joining NVIDIA, I led LLM inference acceleration for AWS Trainium. My teams built the inference platform and application stack for Trainium, and delivered the SDK, kernels and features behind its performance on LLMs such as Llama, GPT-OSS and DeepSeek. I built first-principles performance models of the hardware intrinsics to set the kernel roadmap. I also drove quarterly roadmaps across the inference stack, from inference engines such as vLLM down to kernels, and worked closely with the runtime and collectives teams. We launched multi-core and multi-instance deployments, including disaggregated inference and offloaded prefix caching, using new sharding and parallelism techniques. I also drove the NeuronX Distributed SDK, which gave customers a simple, extensible way to run large language models such as Llama, Mistral and GPT at lower inference cost than comparable GPU-based instances. My teams contributed core capabilities to vLLM, PyTorch XLA and other open source ML libraries.

I received my Master’s in Computer Science from Georgia Tech with a specialization in Machine Learning.

Earlier I worked on research for recommendation systems, which involved comprehending user-provided content, specifically unstructured item and inventory information, to improve information retrieval for eBay search. I led researchers who built and tuned ML models to understand and qualify item and inventory data. That work improved search quality, created a feedback loop that showed sellers how their content affected clarity and conversion, and led to new experiences and workflows at eBay. My primary focus was on applying modern machine learning research to item and inventory comprehension, and on building semi-supervised and supervised ML models for aspect understanding and quality, image quality controls, and the coherence of item inventory measures.

Earlier in my career, I designed and developed eBay’s Identity architecture. I built Identity Infrastructure products such as the Identity Linking Platform, OAuth management, Secure Token Authentication systems and Federated Identity, which formed critical platform components of flows such as checkout and search, with a focus on security and availability. I also built end-to-end solutions for integrating social identity providers into the eBay ecosystem, created a single-use code authentication platform, designed the next generation of API authentication protocols, and redesigned the authentication token data model and Identity enablement systems. During this period I also worked on PCI DSS compliance, encryption standards, OAuth standards, the OpenID Connect protocol, and intelligent ATO prevention and bot attack mitigation.

I also led teams building large-scale distributed systems for eBay sellers. As the engineering leader in Toronto, I managed multiple teams that integrated the Terapeak Research Analytics platform into eBay marketplaces, using a multi-tier architecture with an end-to-end data processing pipeline that gives sellers insights in near real time. Later I managed the Seller Marketing platform, one of the most critical components of the eBay marketplace, with touchpoints across search, view item, checkout and orders. It covered promotion lifecycle management, checkout and cart redemption, and the end-to-end pipeline for promotions, and my teams released Seller Coded Coupons on it.
